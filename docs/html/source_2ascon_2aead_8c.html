Ascon-AEAD128 authenticated encryption.

Encrypts plaintext in place and produces a 128-bit authentication tag over the ciphertext and, when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined, over associated_data as well. The operation follows Algorithm 3 of NIST SP 800-232 Section 4.1.1.

On return, the buffer addressed by plaintext holds the ciphertext of equal length. The original plaintext is overwritten and cannot be recovered from plaintext alone.

Parameters
[in]key128-bit secret key.
[in]nonce128-bit public nonce.
[in,out]plaintextOn entry: plaintext buffer of plaintext_size bytes. On return: ciphertext of equal length, written in place.
[in]plaintext_sizeLength of plaintext in bytes. May be zero, in which case no plaintext blocks are processed and the tag covers only associated data.
[in]associated_dataPointer to associated data of associated_size bytes. Authenticated but not encrypted. Only present when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined.
[in]associated_sizeLength of associated_data in bytes. May be zero. Only present when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined.
[out]authentication_tag128-bit authentication tag produced after finalisation, written as four consecutive 32-bit words.
Precondition
key, nonce, plaintext and authentication_tag are non-NULL.
plaintext points to a buffer of at least plaintext_size bytes.
plaintext and authentication_tag do not overlap.
The nonce is used at most once for a given key.

Ascon-AEAD128 authenticated decryption.

Decrypts ciphertext in place and verifies the 128-bit authentication tag over the plaintext and, when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined, over associated_data as well. The operation follows Algorithm 4 of NIST SP 800-232 Section 4.1.1.

On return, the buffer addressed by ciphertext holds the plaintext of equal length. The original ciphertext is overwritten and cannot be recovered from ciphertext alone.

Parameters
[in]key128-bit secret key.
[in]nonce128-bit public nonce.
[in,out]ciphertextOn entry: ciphertext buffer of ciphertext_size bytes. On return: plaintext of equal length, written in place.
[in]ciphertext_sizeLength of ciphertext in bytes. May be zero, in which case no ciphertext blocks are processed and the tag covers only associated data.
[in]associated_dataPointer to associated data of associated_size bytes. Authenticated but not decrypted. Only present when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined.
[in]associated_sizeLength of associated_data in bytes. May be zero. Only present when VERUM_ASCON_AEAD128_ASSOCIATED_DATA_DEF is defined.
[in]authentication_tag128-bit authentication tag to verify after finalisation, read as four consecutive 32-bit words.
Precondition
key, nonce, ciphertext and authentication_tag are non-NULL.
ciphertext points to a buffer of at least ciphertext_size bytes.
ciphertext and authentication_tag do not overlap.
The nonce matches the one used during encryption.
